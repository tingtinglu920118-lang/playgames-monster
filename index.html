Beginner Survival Tips

  • 1Always keep moving — standing still is a death sentence in the later waves.
  • 2Prioritize area-of-effect upgrades early to handle swarms of smaller enemies.
  • 3Collect XP orbs immediately; letting them sit puts you behind on level-ups.
  • 4Use terrain edges to funnel monster groups into predictable paths.
  • 5Pick one weapon synergy and commit — spreading upgrades dilutes your power.
